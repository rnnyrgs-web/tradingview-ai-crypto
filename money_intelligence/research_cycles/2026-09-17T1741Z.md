## Causal updates

### A. FOMC: path surprise does not map mechanically into persistent cross-asset tightening
Status: SUPPORTED / PROVISIONAL.

Observed sequence: expected 25 bp hike -> hawkish projected path -> initial front-end/USD tightening -> next-session long yields fall, equities and gold rise as oil/inflation pressure eases.

Inference: scheduled-policy price response depends on separable shocks: headline action surprise, expected future path/terminal-rate surprise, growth/inflation information shock, and contemporaneous exogenous shocks such as energy. `Hawkish` is not a sufficient one-factor trade signal.

Next test: build timestamp-safe FOMC sample and compare headline action, path surprise, SEP growth/inflation surprise, 2y/10y decomposition and 30m/1d/5d cross-asset returns.

### B. Sep. 15 funding test weakens the simple aggregate-liquidity scarcity trigger
Status: WEAKENED / REGIME-SPECIFIC.

Pre-event hypothesis: reserves + ON RRP near/below 10% of GDP, combined with estimated-tax payments and Treasury settlements, could cause material repo pressure.

Observed control: median SOFR rose only 2 bp to 3.64% for Sep. 15 transactions and stayed below the old 3.65% IORB. That is inconsistent with broad median secured-funding scarcity despite the high-sensitivity aggregate-liquidity regime.

Inference: the sub-10%-of-GDP Fed-liquidity threshold is better treated as a sensitivity condition than a trigger. Dealer balance-sheet capacity, MMF cash, exact settlement composition, reserve distribution and Fed backstops determine whether the vulnerability actually binds.

Remaining unknown: upper-tail SOFR/TGCR dispersion, standing-repo usage and the next H.4.1 reserve/TGA print. Do not overstate resolution until these are primary-verified.

### C. Physical-energy substitution is asset-specific: crude can ease while system stress persists
Status: SUPPORTED / REGIME-SPECIFIC.

Observed: Saudi crude rerouting via Oman pushed Brent lower even as broader East-West Pipeline damage and extremely low Hormuz traffic were reported.

Inference: `energy shock` must be decomposed by product, route, storage, substitution elasticity, freight/insurance and regional demand destruction. Gross damaged capacity is not the tradable variable; effective deliverable supply is closer.

Pre-move indicators: effective deliverable supply by product; route substitution capacity; storage/inventory location and quality; freight/insurance; physical differentials; futures curves; regional import demand destruction.

### D. QT composition matters independently of policy-rate direction
Status: CANDIDATE.

Observed: BoE held Bank Rate but changed the future pace/composition of gilt runoff/sales.

Hypothesis: maturity composition and active-sale pace of QT can materially affect term premium even when the policy rate is unchanged or hawkish. Test BoE gilt-curve response versus matched policy meetings without QT-design changes.

### E. Crypto ETF outflows + price resilience may indicate absorption, but not yet a bullish signal
Status: CANDIDATE / LOW-MODERATE confidence.

Observed: Farside records two consecutive large BTC ETF outflow sessions (~-$450m and ~-$296m) while BTC remained around mid-$70ks on Sep. 17.

Inference: resilience against observable institutional selling may contain information about offsetting spot demand, but this is not evidence of accumulation until spot depth, basis/funding/OI, exchange flows and subsequent ETF sessions confirm it.

### F. Tokenized-equity infrastructure gained a genuine regulatory catalyst, distinct from generic crypto beta
Status: SUPPORTED STRUCTURAL CATALYST / ASSET-LEVEL EDGE UNKNOWN.

Observed: SEC temporary conditional relief explicitly enables limited onchain trading of tokenized NMS shares through qualifying venues/liquidity pools while preserving underlying shareholder rights.

Inference: this lowers a specific regulatory barrier for tokenized U.S. equity market infrastructure. Beneficiaries should be researched at the infrastructure/venue/custody/settlement layer rather than assuming the news is bullish for all crypto tokens.

Falsifiers: low issuer participation, weak venue adoption/liquidity, legal challenge, adverse final rulemaking, or economics accruing mainly to incumbent brokers/market makers rather than public crypto assets.
