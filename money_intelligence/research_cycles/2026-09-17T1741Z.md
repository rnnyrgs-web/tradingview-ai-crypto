## Causal updates

### A. FOMC: path surprise does not map mechanically into persistent cross-asset tightening
Status: SUPPORTED / PROVISIONAL.

Observed sequence: expected 25 bp hike -> hawkish projected path -> initial front-end/USD tightening -> next-session long yields fall, USD eases, equities and gold rise.

Inference: scheduled-policy price response depends on at least three separable shocks: (1) headline action surprise, (2) expected future path/terminal-rate surprise, and (3) growth/inflation information shock. A hawkish path can coexist with lower long yields if term-premium/growth/inflation interpretation or prior positioning dominates after the first reaction.

Falsification/test: build timestamp-safe FOMC sample and compare headline action, path surprise, SEP growth/inflation surprise, 2y/10y decomposition and 30m/1d/5d cross-asset returns. Do not use `hawkish` as a one-factor trade signal.

### B. Physical-energy substitution is asset-specific: crude can ease while LNG/refined-product scarcity persists
Status: SUPPORTED / REGIME-SPECIFIC.

Observed: Saudi crude rerouting via Oman pushed Brent lower even as East-West Pipeline damage proved broader than first reported; meanwhile LNG prices/import displacement remain extreme.

Inference: `energy shock` must be decomposed by molecule/product, route, storage, substitution elasticity and regional demand destruction. Crude logistics substitution cannot be assumed to repair LNG or refined-product scarcity.

Pre-move indicators: effective deliverable supply by product; route substitution capacity; storage/inventory location and quality; freight/insurance; physical differentials; futures curves; regional import demand destruction.

### C. QT composition matters independently of policy-rate direction
Status: CANDIDATE.

Observed: BoE held Bank Rate but reduced future active gilt-sale pressure, especially long duration, while retaining an eventual balance-sheet unwind.

Hypothesis: maturity composition and active-sale pace of QT can materially affect term premium even when the policy rate is unchanged or hawkish. Test BoE gilt-curve response versus matched policy meetings without QT-design changes.

### D. Crypto ETF outflows + price resilience may indicate absorption, but not yet a bullish signal
Status: CANDIDATE / LOW-MODERATE confidence.

Observed: large reported BTC/ETH ETF redemptions over Sep. 15-16 while BTC remained around mid-$70ks and no broad liquidation cascade was evident in available secondary data.

Inference: price resilience against known institutional selling can be more informative than flow sign alone, but requires confirmation from spot depth, basis/funding/OI, exchange flows and subsequent ETF sessions. Do not promote to BUY without primary flow validation and continuation evidence.
