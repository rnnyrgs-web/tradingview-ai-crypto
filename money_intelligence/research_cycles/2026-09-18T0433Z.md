## Material new evidence

### BOJ expected-hike non-confirmation: rate direction is not flow direction

OBSERVED FACT — The Bank of Japan raised its policy rate 25 bp to 1.25%, a 31-year high, in a 7-2 vote. The move was widely expected before the decision. Japan's August core CPI was 1.7% y/y, with the BOJ-focused ex-fresh-food-and-fuel measure at 1.9%.

OBSERVED FACT — Despite the nominal tightening, the yen weakened after the decision: Reuters reported USD/JPY around 156.91, with the yen down about 0.6% versus the dollar. Reporting attributed the reaction to the already-priced hike, dissent and lack of a sufficiently hawkish signal on the future path.

INFERENCE — This is a fresh matched-control supporting the existing expectation-adjusted-policy-surprise hypothesis from the Sep. 16 FOMC case. The sign of a central-bank action is not enough to infer the sign of the currency move. Marginal information is the realized action/path relative to the pre-event distribution, plus the relative path versus other central banks.

HYPOTHESIS UPDATE — `central-bank tightening -> currency appreciation` is rejected as a standalone causal rule. Better candidate: `expectation-adjusted relative policy-path surprise -> currency repricing`, conditional on positioning, intervention risk and concurrent macro shocks.

FALSIFICATION / next test — Build timestamp-safe event sample across BOJ/FOMC/ECB with pre-event probabilities/OIS, realized action, guidance/path surprise, 30m/1d/5d FX and rates responses. The hypothesis weakens if raw action size/direction explains FX at least as well as expectation-adjusted relative path surprise.

Sources: Reuters Sep. 18 BOJ decision/FX reaction; Reuters Sep. 18 Japan CPI.

OBSERVED FACT — Farside's latest completed table shows U.S. spot BTC ETFs at about -$24.2M on Sep. 17, versus -$450.4M Sep. 15 and -$295.9M Sep. 16. ETH ETFs were about +$3.6M on Sep. 17, versus -$142.0M and -$224.1M on Sep. 15-16 respectively.

INFERENCE — The institutional redemption impulse did not persist at the prior two-day magnitude. This weakens any extrapolation from the Sep. 15-16 outflow cluster into a durable bearish flow regime. It does not establish renewed institutional accumulation because BTC remained slightly negative and one day is insufficient.

DECISION SUPPORT — BTC/ETH remain WAIT / NO EDGE from ETF flows alone. Upgrade only if several sessions of positive flow coincide with improving spot relative strength and healthy derivatives structure; downgrade if large redemptions resume and price fails to absorb them.

Sources: Farside Investors BTC and ETH ETF flow tables, accessed Sep. 18, 2026.
