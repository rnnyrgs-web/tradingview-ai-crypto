# Continuous Research Learning

This layer is advisory research infrastructure only. It learns from genuinely resolved prediction-ledger outcomes, clusters recurring wrong-signal patterns, creates prioritized falsifiable research questions, and stores a bounded memory of prior tested lessons so future Python and AI research cycles avoid blindly repeating low-information work.

It does not mutate production strategies, thresholds, fingerprints, paper history, broker state, live promotions, or trade authority. Any suggested filter, feature, model, or challenger must still be predeclared and pass the canonical chronological validation, untouched OOS, robustness, multiple-testing, point-in-time universe, forward-proof, production-risk, and execution gates.

The lightweight runner combines wrong-signal diagnostics with fixed selective-precision measurement. Research memory stores at most 200 compact lessons and deduplicates by fingerprint. Missing or malformed memory fails closed to an empty advisory state.
