# Continuous Research Learning

Purpose: make research cycles progressively more informed without allowing self-modification to bypass validation.

The learning layer consumes genuine resolved prediction outcomes and produces reusable diagnostics, error clusters, and prioritized falsifiable research questions. It is research-only and cannot change production parameters, authorize trades, promote strategies, reset paper history, or weaken chronological/OOS/forward gates.

Core loop:

resolved outcomes -> error clustering -> research priorities -> predeclared challenger -> backtest -> walk-forward -> untouched OOS -> robustness/cost stress -> forward/shadow evidence -> keep/reject lesson -> repeat

Learning priorities favor recurring wrong-signal clusters with sufficient samples. The system should avoid blindly repeating disproven hypotheses unless market conditions or evidence materially change. Lessons improve research allocation and experiment design; they do not create trade authority.
