No new historical outcomes were opened, no survivor-filtered winners were assembled, and no event/control thresholds were fitted. This preserves untouched outcomes but leaves size/regime-conditioned 2x rarity, matched-control lift, precision@K and calibration unknown for all ten. The committee's ENA revenue backtest is not a matched 2x-event study. Correlated assets, overlapping windows and a news article repeating an issuer release are not independent confirmations. A future move would require separately authenticated formation and realistic executable after-cost evaluation; a candle touching twice the reference is insufficient.

**Genuinely new findings from this sprint:** the AVA purchase-versus-November-issuance reconciliation; a same-window spot/volume/volatility comparison; fresh hourly funding comparisons; completed daily protocol revenue comparisons; and the current USDe activation gap. They are new to this analysis, not claims of original discovery by the market.

**Hypotheses falsified or left unsupported:** (1) AVA's doubled buyback headline implies a large net supply sink: contradicted under the stated constant-pace scenario. (2) ENA's milestone proposal implies active immediate buying: contradicted by the measured supply gap under that schedule. (3) ONDO portfolio subscriptions mechanically buy ONDO: unsupported by product design. (4) ETHFI card spending equals token-holder earnings: category error. (5) All rising assets are washed-out short squeezes: not supported by increasingly positive venue funding, with OI missing. (6) Already realized NEAR/ENA or AVA wick gains were predicted here: categorically false.

**What this teaches about 2x mechanisms:** The useful research distinction is new sustained demand for the *token*, relative to supply actually available for sale. A token can reprice on access and expectations before its business cash flow changes; it can also rally while issuance or leverage makes the move fragile. This cross-section illustrates those mechanisms and missing links. It does not estimate their causal effects or success frequencies. No current case is exceptional enough on the available evidence to promote beyond discovery.

**Single highest-value next experiment:** the AVA net-float-and-execution panel specified above. It resolves a concrete competing explanation with existing/free sources, without starting a new architecture project. No paid product is proposed because free receipt, identity and flow reconciliation must be attempted first; an unverified vendor price would add no scientific value.
