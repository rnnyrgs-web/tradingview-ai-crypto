# Extreme Move Research Factory

## Mission

Find, as early as scientifically possible, every materially tradeable abnormal crypto price move from roughly 5-minute moves through multi-week moves, including rare explosive 2x/3x/5x/10x/20x+ repricings and catastrophic collapses, and continuously improve our understanding of why they happen using only trustworthy point-in-time information available before the move.

The primary objective is genuine sustainable after-cost profitability. Forward BUY/SELL/WAIT precision is secondary and must support, not replace, the profitability objective.

## Core output

The system should not be constrained to a small fixed set of forecast horizons. For each candidate opportunity, research should estimate:

- direction: UP / DOWN / WAIT
- expected move magnitude or distribution
- probability/confidence and calibration
- expected time-to-event or likely duration/window
- after-cost expected value
- liquidity/capacity and execution realism
- invalidation conditions and principal risks
- evidence provenance and forecast-time timestamp integrity

Existing 24h and 7d horizons remain benchmark and validation anchors. Adaptive-horizon selection is itself a research object and must be frozen, chronological, untouched-OOS evaluated, multiple-testing controlled, and prospectively validated before production use.

## Universe policy

Research should scan a broad point-in-time crypto universe rather than only current large caps. This is necessary to avoid survivorship and selection bias and to capture the small/mid-cap assets where extreme repricings often occur.

Do not allocate equal expensive compute to every asset. Use a staged funnel:

1. broad cheap universe scan
2. anomaly / precursor detection
3. candidate ranking
4. intensive feature and hypothesis evaluation
5. tradeability / liquidity / execution filter
6. BUY / SELL / WAIT research output

Universe construction must be point-in-time safe and preserve delisted, failed, illiquid, and non-winning assets when they were genuinely part of the historical opportunity set.

## Big Move Radar

A permanent research lane should search for materially tradeable abnormal moves relative to each asset's own volatility, liquidity, spread, and expected execution cost. A universal percentage threshold must not define all 'big' moves.

The radar should support horizons from approximately 5 minutes through multi-week periods when justified by evidence. Candidate scoring may use, when timestamp-defensible:

- returns, momentum, acceleration, and volatility state
- volume and turnover acceleration
- spread, depth, and liquidity changes
- order-flow / microstructure signals
- cross-exchange dislocations
- market breadth / relative strength
- BTC / ETH leadership and cross-asset state
- funding, open interest, basis, and liquidation conditions
- token supply, float, unlocks, burns, buybacks
- exchange listings/delistings and venue expansion
- holder concentration and verifiable wallet/exchange flows
- on-chain usage, protocol activity, fees, revenue
- developer activity
- search/social attention and attention acceleration
- news, regulatory, partnership, product, and security catalysts
- regime interactions and combinations of genuinely independent signals

No feature is presumed useful merely because it is plausible. Every feature family must earn inclusion through timestamp-safe incremental OOS evidence after realistic costs.

## Extreme Move Watch

Maintain a dedicated tail-research lane for upside moves of at least 2x and severe downside collapses.

For upside, explicitly estimate tail probabilities when scientifically supportable:

- P(move >= 2x)
- P(move >= 3x)
- P(move >= 5x)
- P(move >= 10x)
- P(move >= 20x)

For downside, study severe collapses such as approximately:

- -50%
- -80%
- -95%

These are research targets, not promises. Low-data tails must remain explicitly uncertain and fail closed when evidence is insufficient.

## Historical extreme-event laboratory

Build and continuously expand a historical event library of extreme winners, extreme losers, and matched controls.

For every event:

1. define the event timestamp and objective outcome without using post-event information to choose pre-event features
2. reconstruct only information genuinely available before the move
3. identify candidate precursors and mechanisms
4. construct matched controls that looked similar before the event but did not subsequently make the extreme move
5. test whether the proposed precursor discriminates events from controls
6. test how early the information became available
7. test whether the apparent edge survives realistic liquidity, spread, slippage, funding, missed fills, and capacity assumptions
8. freeze any resulting hypothesis before OOS evaluation
9. perform chronological non-overlapping testing
10. use untouched OOS and multiple-testing protection
11. require prospective/forward evidence before promotion
12. preserve negative and null results in durable research memory

Single famous winners such as PONS may be used only as case studies. They must never become templates that are fit retrospectively and then presented as generalizable evidence.

## Crash laboratory

Treat major downside events as a first-class research target rather than merely the mirror image of pumps. Study pre-crash conditions including, when timestamp-safe:

- leverage and open-interest build-up
- funding/basis dislocations
- liquidation sensitivity and concentration
- liquidity withdrawal / spread expansion / depth loss
- exchange inflows and abnormal sell pressure
- holder concentration and distribution
- token unlock / float expansion
- security incidents and exploit precursors
- regulatory or venue risk
- cross-asset stress transmission
- volatility and correlation regime shifts
- failed breakouts / momentum exhaustion
- market-wide versus idiosyncratic crash structure

Require matched non-crash controls and prospective validation exactly as for upside extremes.

## Early Warning layer

Research an EARLY WARNING output that is distinct from a trade signal. It should answer whether the system detects an unusual build-up of evidence before a potentially extreme move while remaining explicit about uncertainty.

An Early Warning candidate should contain:

- asset
- timestamp
- suspected direction
- plausible move-size distribution
- likely time window / duration range
- evidence families contributing
- contradictory evidence
- confidence/calibration state
- tradeability/liquidity state
- invalidation criteria
- whether evidence is sufficient for research-only escalation or still WAIT

Early Warning must not bypass normal promotion, execution, or risk gates.

## Adaptive-horizon science

Do not choose the best horizon after observing the outcome. Any horizon-selection rule must be predeclared/frozen before OOS use.

Acceptable research approaches may include:

- time-to-threshold / survival models
- competing-risk UP vs DOWN event models
- multi-horizon probability curves
- magnitude-by-time conditional distributions
- volatility-normalized event barriers
- predeclared hierarchical horizon selection

The horizon-selection mechanism must itself be evaluated for leakage, multiple-testing inflation, stability, calibration, and after-cost usefulness.

## Division of labor

### ChatGPT Lead Integrator

- owns global profitability-first prioritization
- reads the shared state and prevents duplication across AI engines and worker lanes
- selects the highest-value unresolved research questions
- synthesizes evidence and decides what should be tested next
- ensures the adaptive-horizon / extreme-move mission remains aligned with canonical safety and scientific gates

### Claude

- performs deep scientific research and hypothesis discovery
- studies historical extreme events and matched controls
- searches for mechanisms and point-in-time predictive information
- produces precise falsifiable research specifications rather than unsupported stories

### Claude adversarial reviewer

- independently tries to falsify proposed extreme-move predictors
- searches for winner-selection bias, retrospective narratives, leakage, control mismatch, insufficient samples, and unrealistic execution
- specifies decisive falsification experiments

### Claude Code

- implements frozen experiment specifications
- builds data pipelines, features, event-labeling, matched-control logic, backtests, tests, and research artifacts on isolated branches
- does not self-promote strategies or bypass scientific review

### Existing quantitative workers

- perform bulk deterministic scans, backtests, robustness checks, cohort construction, diagnostics, and prospective evidence accumulation
- should handle high-volume compute so expensive AI reasoning is reserved for high-information-value work

## Required scientific controls

Never claim improvement through:

- lookahead or timestamp leakage
- using future exchange listings/universe membership
- survivorship bias
- selecting only memorable winners
- post-outcome feature construction
- train/validation/OOS contamination
- repeated reuse of untouched OOS
- overlapping events treated as independent
- parameter or horizon mining
- uncorrected multiple testing
- unrealistic execution assumptions
- removing non-tradeable historical failures from the universe
- changing a fingerprint after observing OOS results

Require, as applicable:

- point-in-time universe reconstruction
- event de-duplication and non-overlap rules
- matched controls
- chronological splits
- frozen fingerprints
- untouched OOS
- cost and liquidity stress
- stability across time / assets / regimes
- uncertainty intervals
- calibrated probabilities
- multiple-testing protection
- prospective/forward cohorts

## Promotion and execution safety

This factory is research-only until canonical promotion requirements are met.

It may autonomously scan, hypothesize, test, reject, remember, and rank candidates. It may not:

- authorize real-money trading
- connect or operate a broker
- weaken WAIT/abstention
- fabricate missing evidence
- rewrite authentic paper or forward history
- auto-promote a strategy without canonical approval
- bypass exact-head Security & Reliability or other required repository gates

Missing, stale, malformed, future, ambiguous, or scientifically insufficient evidence must fail closed.

## Cost discipline

Use the existing approximately $30/month combined variable paid-AI/infrastructure ceiling unless explicitly changed by the owner.

Expensive AI reasoning should focus on hypothesis discovery, causal/mechanistic analysis, adversarial review, and high-value synthesis. Broad scans, cohort construction, labeling, backtests, deterministic diagnostics, and evidence accumulation should preferentially use local/free/existing workers.

Paid-AI throttling must not stop deterministic research, data collection, paper trading, signal generation, diagnostics, dashboards, scans, logging, or evidence accumulation.

10. Surface research-only dashboard outputs only after their provenance, calibration state, and fail-closed semantics are explicit.

## Success criterion

Success is not a large number of alerts, experiments, or retrospective explanations. Success is a small number of reproducible, timestamp-safe, prospectively validated signals that increase genuine sustainable after-cost profitability while correctly abstaining when the evidence is weak.
